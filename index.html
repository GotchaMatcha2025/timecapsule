The personal data collected through this Time Capsule experience is managed by Gotcha Matcha ("we", "us", "our"), operating in Singapore. For data-related enquiries, contact us at: hello@gotchamatcha.com.

2. Personal Data We Collect

When you participate in this experience, we collect the following personal data:

  • Your full name
  • Your email address
  • Your video recording (up to 15 seconds), including your image, likeness, and voice
  • The date and time of your submission

3. Purpose of Collection

Your personal data is collected and used solely for the following purposes:

  • Time Capsule delivery — to store your video and return it to you approximately one year after the date of recording, via email notification or QR code retrieval.
  • Marketing and promotional use — your video, image, likeness, and/or voice may be used by Gotcha Matcha in marketing materials, social media content, brand campaigns, event recaps, or promotional videos, without further notice or additional compensation.   • Internal analytics — aggregate, anonymised data (e.g. number of participants) may be used to evaluate the event experience.

We will not use your personal data for any purpose beyond those stated above without obtaining your separate consent.

4. Disclosure of Personal Data

Your personal data may be disclosed to the following parties only to the extent necessary to fulfil the stated purposes:

  • Supabase Inc. — our cloud storage and database provider, used to securely store your video and submission data. Supabase operates under its own privacy policy and data processing terms.
  • Service providers engaged by Gotcha Matcha for marketing production, subject to confidentiality obligations.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to any third party for their own commercial use.

5. Retention Period

Your personal data will be retained for a period of no longer than 2 years from the date of submission, after which it will be securely deleted. Your video will be made available to you for retrieval for one year from the date of submission, after which it will be archived or deleted at our discretion.

6. Your Rights Under the PDPA

As a data subject under Singapore's PDPA, you have the right to:

  • Access your personal data held by us
  • Correct any inaccurate personal data
  • Withdraw consent at any time, subject to legal or contractual restrictions — note that withdrawal of consent does not affect the lawfulness of any use made prior to withdrawal

To exercise any of the above rights, contact us at hello@gotchamatcha.com. We will respond within 30 days of receiving your request.

Please note that withdrawing consent for marketing use will not result in the deletion of your time capsule video unless you also request data erasure explicitly.

7. Protection of Personal Data

We take reasonable security measures to protect your personal data from unauthorised access, disclosure, modification, or destruction. Your video is stored in a secured cloud environment accessible only via a unique, non-guessable link tied to your email address.

8. Voluntariness of Consent

Your participation in this experience is entirely voluntary. However, as the collection of your name, email, and video is necessary to fulfil the core purpose of the Time Capsule, you will not be able to participate if you do not provide consent.

Your browser needs camera and microphone access to record your message.

📱 iPhone — Safari

  1. Open the Settings app
  2. Scroll down to Safari
  3. Under Privacy & Security → set Camera to Allow
  4. Come back here and tap Retry

📱 Android — Chrome

  1. Tap the lock icon in the address bar
  2. Tap Permissions
  3. Turn on Camera and Microphone
  4. Refresh the page, then tap Retry

Allow in your browser

  1. Look for a camera or lock icon in your address bar
  2. Tap it and select Allow for camera and microphone
  3. Reload the page and try again
